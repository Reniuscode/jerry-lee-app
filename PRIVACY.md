# Jerry Lee: privacy policy

Jerry Lee is a private assistant used only by its owner. It has no other users.

- **What it accesses:** the owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar, with read-only permission. It cannot send, change or delete email or calendar events.
- **Where data goes:** data is processed on the owner's own computer. To answer the owner's requests, relevant content may be sent to the AI model provider the owner has chosen (Google Gemini API, paid tier). Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.
- **Storage:** sign-in tokens and any notes are stored only on the owner's computer.
- **Removing access:** the owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Last updated: 30 September 2026.
